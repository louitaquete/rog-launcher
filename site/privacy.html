Privacy Policy / Política de privacidade

Last updated: November 2026

Summary

Rog Launcher does not collect, sell or share personal data. There is no telemetry, analytics or tracking. Everything the launcher stores stays on your computer.

Data stored on your computer

  • Your Microsoft/Minecraft session tokens, saved in %APPDATA%\.Rog so you don't have to log in every time. You can remove them at any time with "Sign out".
  • Your instances, settings, skins library and downloaded game files.

Services the launcher talks to

To work, the launcher connects directly from your computer to: Microsoft and Xbox Live (login), Mojang/Minecraft services (game files, profile, skins and capes), Modrinth and CurseForge (mods and modpacks), Fabric, Quilt, Forge and NeoForge (loaders) and GitHub (updates). Their own privacy policies apply to those requests. Rog Launcher has no servers of its own.

Website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and does not use cookies or analytics.
